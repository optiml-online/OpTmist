02 — A DIFFERENT HABIT

The aerator
saves water.
You save the rest.

OpTmist is a reminder you touch every day. Pair it with four small habits and water saving stops being an effort; it becomes how you live. 

01

Close it while you soap.

Wet your hands, turn the tap off, lather, then rinse. Avoid letting water run while it isn't needed.

02

Half-open is enough.

Try the lowest comfortable setting for the task, rather than opening the tap fully by default.

03

Notice the sound.

A softer, controlled flow can be a cue to use water more deliberately.

04

Teach the next person.

Share the habit with everyone at home. Consistency is what makes small changes add up.

06 — GOOD TO KNOW

Asked often.

A few practical answers before you choose an aerator.

Why one per household?_*

This is not about limited stock or asking you to make do with one. It is about starting with the tap where everyone washes their hands before and after meals. OpTmist is more than a water-saving attachment: it is a daily reminder that we often need less water than we think. Over time, that awareness can become a habit you carry to every other tap and shower, even where OpTmist is not fitted.

Which tap should it fit on?

The included guide references F22 (22 mm) and M24 (24 mm). Faucet designs vary, so please verify the thread and fit before ordering.
